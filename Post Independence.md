POST INDEPENDENCE

Perhaps a good place to start is understanding the processes that took place in the 20th century, before, during and after independence. 
Of course, another historian may want to go further than this. 
But looking at this historical window helps us understand and answer some key questions. Essentially, my aim is to help us all wrap our minds around these key questions -

What state was India in before Independence?
How did it get there?
What challenges lay during and after independence?

Perhaps, as a starter, let us consider the data compiled by economic historian Angus Maddison. A brief introduction to Maddison can be viewed here - https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/historicaldevelopment/maddison/original-maddison
